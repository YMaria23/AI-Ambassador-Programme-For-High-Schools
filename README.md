# AI-Ambassador-Programme-For-High-Schools
At the initiative of my Artificial Intelligence teacher, I held a presentation regarding the introduction of high school pupils to the vast concept of AI. This repository has all the materials that I used.

- The ppt presentation -> contains introductory AI concepts; explains what kind of problems AI solves and why it appeared in the first place

- additional pdf -> contains responses collected form and ice-breaker quiz, regarding how high school students use AI in their daily life

- laboratories from university, regading object detection, regression and classification problems
